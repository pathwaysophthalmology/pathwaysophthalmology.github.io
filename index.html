Eye movements, visual pathways and pupils

A short neuro-ophthalmology course for finals and the UKMLA. Move the eye, cut the visual pathway and examine the pupils, so that the anatomy explains the signs, then use it to localise lesions in clinical cases and OSCE stations.

Pathways is a free, short online course on eye movements, the visual pathway and the pupils. A baseline test at the start and a final test at the end show how far you have come, and a certificate is available when you finish.

What the course covers

  • Eye movements: the six extraocular muscles, the nine positions of gaze, and third, fourth and sixth cranial nerve palsies
  • The visual pathway: lesions from the optic nerve to the occipital cortex and the visual field defects they cause
  • Pupil reflexes: the pupillary light reflex and the sympathetic pathway to the pupil
  • The abnormal pupil: relative afferent pupillary defect (RAPD), Horner syndrome, Adie pupil and third nerve palsy
  • Clinical cases on localising the lesion
  • OSCE examination stations: eye movements, pupils and visual fields
  • A baseline test and a final test, with every question explained
  • A certificate of completion

Who it is for

UK medical students revising for finals and the UKMLA or preparing for the Duke Elder Undergraduate Prize Examination, and foundation doctors who want to refresh their neuro-ophthalmology.
